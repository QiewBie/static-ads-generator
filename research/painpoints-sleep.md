# Sleep / Wind-down — micro-pain taxonomy (2026-06-23)

Product fit: **tea Night** (Reishi · chamomile · lavender · caffeine-free). **Compliance spine:** Night = *parasympathetic wind-down ritual*, NEVER sedation — never "fall asleep faster" / "knocks you out." The allowed register is calmer wind-down, a quieter mind, fewer 2am wake-ups, deep-sleep architecture. (Tea is currently a back-burner product — this is research-ahead.) Source-weighted pain-first (r/sleep, r/insomnia, r/ADHD, r/Anxiety) + the melatonin-quit competitor layer.

> **Same three axes:** frequency × specificity × **fit/compliance.** Sleep is where fit does the most filtering — several frequent, vivid pains are un-ownable without a sedation claim we forbid.

---

## Micro-pain 1: The mind that won't shut off at lights-out  ·  frequency: HIGH  ·  fit: STRONGEST
- **Persona:** overthinker / ADHD adult / stressed professional, in bed, lights out.
- **Situation:** the body is exhausted but the mind loops over everything — replays the day, tomorrow's list — for 40+ minutes. They already try "sleepytime tea" and it isn't enough.
- **Real voice:** "My mind just has nonstop thoughts about any and everything" (r/sleep) · "My brain won't let me fall asleep… How do I make it shut up?" (r/ADHD) · "mind is racing and looping over" · "I listened to a sleep meditation for 40 minutes with my eyes [still open]" (r/ADHD).
- **Angle seed:** "Your body's done. Your brain didn't get the memo." / the wind-down that finally quiets the loop.
- **Alcami fit:** the Night ritual's exact job — parasympathetic calm to soften the mental noise. **Skeptic reframe is built in:** "You've had bedtime tea. It didn't do this." Competitor-quit: vs. plain sleepytime tea.

## Micro-pain 2: Wired but exhausted — tired all day, wide awake at 11pm  ·  frequency: HIGH  ·  fit: GOOD
- **Persona:** 9–5 / screen worker; bed has become a place of vigilance.
- **Situation:** dead tired by evening, but the moment they lie down they're wired — the body never got the signal to switch from work-mode to rest.
- **Real voice:** "i'm exhausted every day but can't sleep at night" (r/ADHD) · "wired but exhausted" (r/sleep) · "Your brain now associates bed with work and vigilance, rather than relaxation."
- **Angle seed:** "Exhausted all day. Wide awake at 11pm."
- **Alcami fit:** the evening ritual that marks the shift out of work-mode; parasympathetic. **Don't claim to fix the pattern** — sell the ritual that signals "day's over." Competitor-quit: low.

## Micro-pain 3: The 3am wake-up that won't release you  ·  frequency: HIGH  ·  fit: CAREFUL
- **Persona:** light sleeper, mid-30s+.
- **Situation:** wakes at 3–4am most nights and can't get back down until 7–8am; a cortisol-surge pattern, brutal before a 6am alarm.
- **Real voice:** "Why do I keep waking up at 3am and can't fall back asleep?" (r/sleep) · "I wake up at 3-4am and I can't fall asleep anymore until 7 or 8am… at least 4 days out of seven I have to get up at 6" (r/insomnia) · several take melatonin and "make sure there's at least 8 hours."
- **Angle seed:** the 3am ceiling / "I stopped buying melatonin" (melatonin-quit).
- **Alcami fit:** **careful** — only the brief's sanctioned framing ("fewer 2am wake-ups, deep-sleep architecture"), never "fixes 3am waking." Strong **melatonin-quit** comparison angle. Test, don't lead.

## Micro-pain 4 (DROP): Partner's snoring keeps me awake  ·  frequency: HIGH · specificity: HIGH · fit: NONE
- Frequent and vivid ("snores so loud I can't sleep," "ruining my life," "I'm a very light sleeper so I get no sleep") — **but un-ownable.** We can't fix a partner's airway, and "sleep deep enough to not hear it" is a sedation claim we forbid. **The frequency filter alone would have kept this; the fit axis correctly kills it.** Useful as the textbook example of why fit gates frequency.

---
**Wave-one picks:** #1 (racing mind) and #2 (wired-but-exhausted) — highest fit, fully compliant on the parasympathetic line. #3 is a careful melatonin-quit test. #4 is the discipline check: frequent ≠ ownable.
