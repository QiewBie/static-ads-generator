# Claim verification — "caffeine-free" (Creamy Mushroom Latte Blend)

**Verdict:** The blend is **caffeine-free for the Original SKU** — a brand-stated, first-party claim. "Zero caffeine / caffeine-free" is safe in Original creative. The **Matcha** flavor carries **minimal caffeine** (not zero), and Cacao carries trace theobromine — so an absolute caffeine-free claim is Original-only. Creative is Original-only regardless, so the claim holds.

**Sources (first-party, Tavily-pulled):**
- alcamielements.com (homepage): "Calm steady energy without caffeine crashes or overstimulation." · "No Caffeine" · "Caffeine Free" · "a smooth, balanced alternative to high-caffeine drinks."
- Product page (alcami-elements-life-enhancing-beverage): "Caffeine Free"; the Matcha variant is described as "high-grade Japanese matcha … with minimal caffeine."
- Official Instagram: "Caffeine-Free Energy Without The Jitters" · "Caffeine-Free • NON-GMO • Dairy-Free • Keto • Vegan • Adaptogens."
- Alcami blog "Mushroom Coffee for Anxiety": positions the blend as a caffeine-free alternative.

**Caveat noted for guards:** Amazon lists the product as a "Mushroom Coffee Enhancer" ("smooth out caffeine") — an add-to-coffee use case, not a statement that the product contains caffeine. The standalone DTC product is caffeine-free.

**Why it matters:** the sleep campaign rests on caffeine-displacement (swap the afternoon coffee for a caffeine-free warm cup). That premise is now verified at the product level, not just assumed from audience pain threads. The honest sleep story stays upstream (remove the caffeine cause) — never a sedation/sleep-aid claim.
