# Meta Ad Copy Conventions — Primary Text / Headline / Description
**For:** Alcami Elements ad-copy work (the Ads Manager text fields, NOT in-image text)
**Evidence:** 28 live primary texts pulled June 2026 via Scrape Creators (AG1 page `183869772601`, IM8 page `345914995276546`) + Meta mechanics verified via Tavily.

---

## 1. How the fields mechanically work

- **Primary text:** ~125 characters visible on mobile feed before "…See more". The first line is the only guaranteed-seen copy — it must work standalone. Everything after the fold is for the reader who already cared enough to tap.
- **Up to 5 primary-text variants + 5 headline variants per ad.** Advantage+ picks the variant per user. Copy is a *portfolio*, not a single "correct" text — short and long variants of the same ad coexist and serve different readers.
- **Headline:** ~40 chars display. **Description:** ~30 chars, not shown on all placements — treat as a bonus proof slot, never load-bearing.
- **Meta low-quality policy:** *excessive* capitalization and *excessive* emoji use trigger ad rejection or delivery suppression. Single-word caps and functional emoji use are safe and standard.

## 2. What the category's biggest spenders actually run (28 live texts)

**The barbell.** Both AG1 and IM8 run two registers simultaneously, with almost nothing in between:
- **Ultra-short:** one plain sentence, zero emoji ("Since 2021, AG1 has been the morning ritual…", "AG1 is rigorously tested for quality and third-party verified.")
- **Long structured direct-response:** 80–250 words with a consistent architecture.

**The long-copy architecture (consistent across both brands):**
```
HOOK LINE (fits above the fold, often with 1 emoji-bracket pair on launches: 🚨 X is here 🚨)
[blank line]
1–3 sentence explainer paragraph
[blank line]
emoji-bulleted benefit block:     💚 benefit  ⚡ benefit  🥄 benefit
offer block:                      ✅ FREE item  ✅ FREE item
[blank line]
fine print († study disclaimer, ** offer terms, FDA disclaimer)
```

**Emoji rules in the wild:** emojis are *functional anchors* — bullet markers (✅✔️💚⚡), or one bracket-pair on an announcement line (🔥…🔥 / 🚨…🚨). Never sprinkled mid-sentence as decoration. Quiet brand-voice one-liners carry zero emoji.

**Caps in the wild:** single words only — FREE, HERE, NEW. Zero full-caps sentences across all 28 ads. "Screaming" is done by *line structure and specificity*, not capitalization.

**Long advertorial works.** IM8 runs 200-word PAS essays (joint pain, gut-serotonin, supplement-form quality) as feed ads at scale — proof the category sustains narrative copy when the first line hooks.

**Proof lives in the description slot:** "50k verified 5-star reviews" (AG1), "Co-founded by David Beckham. Backed by science." (IM8).

**IM8's GLP-1 copy** names the class openly in headlines ("What GLP-1 Strips Away, IM8 Restores") and uses heavy second-person ("your gut", "when you come off, the weight comes back"). They accept the Meta personal-attributes risk; **Alcami deliberately doesn't** — our third-person product-fit rule stays (it's a real policy line: ads may not assert/imply knowledge of a user's medical condition).

## 3. What this means for Alcami copy

Our own live ad ("Tired of running on empty?" / "Anti-Lazy Formula" / 200k proof) is already louder DR than a "premium restrained" register — and the parents-batch feedback said the same thing from the client's mouth: plain-words specific outcome + proof beat clever brand poetry.

**The register tiers for Alcami primary text:**

| Register | When | Emoji | Caps | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Quiet one-liner** | scene/editorial/poster ads, tea | none | none | 1 sentence |
| **Outcome voice** | UGC, testimonial, parents/GLP-1-style specific outcomes | 0–2 native | none | 1–3 sentences |
| **Structured DR** | blocks, comparison, offer/launch ads (blend acquisition) | functional bullets ✅⚡🍄🌿☕ + max 1 bracket-pair on the hook line | single words (FREE, NEW) | hook + paragraphs + bullet block |
| **Advertorial PAS** | cold structured ads where the mechanism is the story | sparse or none | none | 80–200 words, blank-line paragraphs |

**Portfolio rule:** write 2–3 primary-text variants per ad spanning at least two registers (one short, one structured); Meta serves the right one per user.

**Unchanged hard lines:** claims literal-true (loud ≠ inflated), no full-caps sentences, no emoji spam, no fake scarcity, no celebrity names, GLP-1 third-person only + no drug brands, coffee never the product noun, price never the hook, tea register stays observed/quiet.
