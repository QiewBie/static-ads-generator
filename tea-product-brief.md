The tea canisters are a completely different visual object from the latte pouches. Each is a **premium cylindrical cardboard tube** (~4.5" tall, ~2.5" diameter) with a distinct color world and a shared botanical illustration.

## Retargeting = Launching to People Who Already Love Us

**Tea retargeting is a PRODUCT LAUNCH to existing blend customers — not an education or proof campaign.** They already trust Alcami and have a ritual. The job is to **share exciting news and extend the relationship**, not to convince. The emotion is belonging and anticipation ("our brand made something new"), not evaluation.

**The four launch angles (in priority):**
1. **"We made tea now!"** — pure announcement energy. They care that *Alcami* made it, not which mushroom is inside.
2. **"Loved the blend? Try this."** — the cleanest cross-sell. Make it specific: "Loved your morning blend? Now there's a tea for 3pm." Extends the ritual — NEVER gap-fills (no "what your ritual is missing").
3. **Two-product display** — blend + tea in one frame. Recognition, not persuasion: "same family, you already own one." (Use `extra_refs` to bring the blend pouch into a tea scene.)
4. **Insider / early access** — reward loyalty: "Our customers get it first." Keep date-free in the image (no "Pre-Order", no ship dates) even though the site is currently pre-order.

**Format fit:** lead with `scene` (two-product / announcement) and emotional `editorial`/`color_block` design. `blocks`/`how_it_works` are the FOLLOW-UP for people who want detail. NEVER `comparison` (launch register is news, not an argument — don't pit our own new product in a vs. layout for people who already buy from us), and never `ugc`, `testimonial`, or `social_proof` — warm buyers don't need authenticity signals or proof. (The validator enforces this; see `python3 gen.py --policy`.)

**Blend↔tea are complementary, never competitors** — tea at first light, latte after breakfast. The Welcome Kit even ships 5 blend travel packs with the tea, a literal bridge between the lines.

## Creative Positioning — What Changes vs Blend Ads

### The central difference

**Blend ads villain:** Fragmentation — too many products, too complicated. Alcami collapses the stack.

**Tea ads villain:** Mismatch — using the same herb all day as if the body's needs don't change. The right herb at the wrong hour doesn't help.

### Angles that work for tea

**1. Circadian precision** — time-specificity stops the scroll. "7am. Cordyceps." "3pm. Lion's Mane." "9pm. Reishi." is a complete ad.

**2. Not just another tea** — "Regular tea: mild relaxation or caffeine. Low potency, mostly flavor. Often contains microplastics." vs. "Alcami Ritual: designed for nervous system regulation. Structured for morning, afternoon, and night."

**3. The brewing ritual as a pause** — "Before the first sip. The pause is half the point. No phone." This angle doesn't exist in any supplement ad. Tea drinkers value the few-minute steep as a moment, not a chore.

**4. A day with rhythm** — "Most people don't have an energy problem. They have a rhythm problem." The strongest system-level hook for all three blends together.

**5. Trifecta as the experiment** — "All three. Three weeks. Let the body decide." The Trifecta entry narrative works for curious buyers and gifters.

**6. Measurable sleep proof** — "+18% HRV." "2am wake-ups stopped in 10 days." This level of specificity is rare in functional tea advertising.

### Tone calibration

Tea ads are **quieter and more precise** than blend ads. The blend declares. The tea observes.

| Blend register | Tea register |
|---|---|
| "YOUR COFFEE IS MISSING 9 THINGS." | "7am. Cordyceps. The slow-rising kind." |
| Condensed ultra-bold confrontational | High-contrast editorial serif or light italic |
| Relief — the moment exhaustion ends | Attunement — the day has a shape |
| Loud, declarative | Quiet, precise, inevitable |

### What NOT to do in tea ads
- Do NOT use "everything at once" — that is the blend's line
- Do NOT lead with price
- Do NOT position tea as a replacement for the blend (they are complementary — tea at first light, latte after breakfast)
- Do NOT promise sedation for Night — it's parasympathetic permission, not a knockout
- Do NOT say Morning is caffeinated — it is caffeine-free, the lift is Cordyceps
- Do NOT use 200,000+ customers — that figure belongs to the blend
- Do NOT use "Try all three" as a Trifecta CTA — it implies 3 separate products. The Trifecta IS one product. Use "Get the Trifecta." / "Begin here." / "Start the ritual." instead
- Do NOT put review counts or customer numbers in generated ad images — use only the star rating (4.9★) or "thousands" if social proof is needed visually
- Do NOT use "From $39/month" — that's the blend price. Tea is "From $37.40/month"

## Strategic Positioning — The Deeper Frame

**The category Alcami can own: rhythm.**

Most wellness brands sell ingredients. Some sell outcomes. Very few sell rhythm. Alcami Tea Ritual can own the idea that the human body moves through distinct states across a day — and that the right intervention at the right hour changes the entire arc.

This is not symptom correction. It is state-transition design.

**The three transitions:**

| Blend | From → To | NOT this |
|---|---|---|
| **Morning** | Inertia → momentum | "Energy tea" |
| **Afternoon** | Overstimulation → clarity | "Focus tea" |
| **Night** | Accumulation → exhale | "Sleep tea" |

Framing as "energy / focus / sleep" collapses the Alcami philosophy back into compartmentalized wellness. Framing as state transitions is emotionally sophisticated and category-defining.

**The system name that fits:**

> The Alcami Ritual System — daily state architecture.

Not supplements. Not performance hacks. A ritual system for how the body moves through a day.

**Why tea makes this land harder than the blend:**

Tea already carries ritual, slowness, repetition, nervous system regulation, and time-of-day behavior. The blend asks people to adopt a new ritual. The tea steps into rituals they already have — morning quiet, afternoon pause, evening wind-down — and gives them structure. The 4-minute steep is not inconvenience; it is the point. People slow down enough to actually feel the ritual working.

**The human truth underneath:**

People are temporally broken. Scrolling at midnight. Wired in the afternoon. Exhausted in the morning. Burned out but unable to slow down. The wellness industry diagnoses symptoms. Tea Ritual addresses the architecture of the day itself.

## Visual Direction — Atmospheric, Not Decorative

Tea visual world is lighter and more atmospheric than the core blend. The blend is dense, creamy, foundational. Tea is directional, environmental, temporal.

**Per-SKU atmosphere:**

| SKU | Light quality | Mood | Visual elements |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Morning** | Low-angle sunlight, warm gold, slight haze | Movement beginning, freshness, quiet activation | Steam rising, sachet steeping, window light, hands around warm cup |
| **Afternoon** | Diffused green light, air, openness | Mental openness, creative flow, no friction | Sage-green world, clean surfaces, the cup mid-afternoon on a clear desk |
